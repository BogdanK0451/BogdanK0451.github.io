Poly Undercut

07A · EXTENSION · NOV 2026

Chrome extension that sets a limit price one tick better than the best price on a prediction market

Shots

  1. BK 4001A
  2. BK 4002A

Poly Undercut adds one button under the limit price field on a prediction market's trade panel. On the Buy tab it sets the price one step above the best bid; on the Sell tab, one step below the best ask. If that would cross the spread, it joins the best price instead, so the order always stays a maker order. It reads the open order book on the page, checks that the book shows the same outcome as the panel, and follows the book's tick size. It only fills the price: the trader still reviews and places the order.

Highlights

  • One click: 'Beat bid' or 'Beat ask' with the exact price on the button
  • Never crosses the spread: switches to 'Join bid' or 'Join ask' when a step would take liquidity
  • Checks the book shows the same outcome as the panel, and follows 1¢ or 0.1¢ ticks
  • Step setting in the popup: automatic tick or a fixed number of cents
  • Fills the field only; it never places an order
