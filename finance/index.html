Finance reads yearly budget Google Sheets (read-only), keeps a synced copy in local SQLite and turns it into savings rate, category trends, outliers, net worth and a year-end forecast. Every number explains what it means and how it is calculated, and the app reconciles its totals against the sheets and shows where they differ. The same app ships as an Android app that runs its API and database entirely on the phone, with Google sign-in and a biometric app lock.

Highlights

  • Incremental sync of a decade of yearly sheets, with reconciliation checks against the sheets' own totals
  • Trends, lifestyle creep, outliers, net worth and a December forecast with its historical miss rate
  • Inflation-adjusted view ('in today's prices') next to the amounts as recorded
  • Ask page: plain-language questions answered by an LLM writing read-only SQL
  • One codebase runs on desktop (Node + SQLite) and fully offline on Android (sql.js in a WebView)
