A Chrome side-panel extension for KupujemProdajem, Serbia's biggest classifieds site. You drop in photos, DeepSeek describes the item and asks a few short questions, then writes a plain, honest ad you can edit. One click fills the site's posting form, including category, photos, price and options, and stops before publishing. It can also save your existing ads and repost them for free when they expire.

Highlights

  • Photo-to-ad flow: item analysis, up to 5 follow-up questions, then a short ad that doesn't read like AI
  • Fills the real form: KP's category suggestions ranked by DeepSeek, photos injected as files, rich-text description
  • Reports field by field what got filled, and never publishes on its own
  • Saves your ads with their photos and reposts them for free on expiry, with badge and notification reminders
  • No backend: the API key and saved ads stay in the browser
