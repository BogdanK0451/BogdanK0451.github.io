Phone app that forecasts rushes at Belgrade airport's security checkpoint, in lanes

Talas forecasts when departing passengers will crowd the central carry-on security checkpoint at Belgrade airport, and how heavy each rush is, measured in lanes open. It turns the day's departures into passengers by aircraft seats, load factor and transfer share, spreads them over an arrival curve in 5-minute slots and groups the result into waves. A staff view adds the load in lanes, a shift summary and quick lane logging, which will later be used to calibrate the model. It is a mobile-only PWA backed by a Cloudflare Worker with scheduled refreshes and push alerts.

Highlights

  • Forecast model: flights → passengers → 5-minute slots → lanes' worth → waves, with a range band
  • Next rush first: time window, flight count and crowd level visible without a tap
  • Staff view: load in lanes, lanes chart, shift summary and one-tap lane logging
  • Fits a free API plan: four flight-data refreshes a day with a monthly quota guard
  • Planes page with filters, a destinations map and a side view of the live sky; a weekly heatmap after four weeks of data
  • Push alerts (evening summary, heads-up before each wave), offline cache, Serbian and English
