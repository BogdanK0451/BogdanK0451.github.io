Expense Voice is a small Android app for logging spending by voice: tap the mic, say something like 'groceries 1500 dinars', check the preview and save. The recognised text goes to the owner's own Google Apps Script, which works out category, amount in the home currency and date; nothing is written until confirmed, and one tap undoes the last row. Version 4 is a from-scratch Kotlin rebuild of an older app whose source was lost, recovered from the decoded APK and redesigned.

Highlights

  • Rebuilt from a decoded APK after the original source was lost
  • One explicit state machine for record, review, save and undo, fixing the old app's overlapping-action crashes
  • Zero third-party dependencies; tiny signed release APK
  • 47 unit tests plus an emulator script that captures every state in light and dark
  • Multilingual speech (English, Serbian, Russian) and seven home currencies
